Room view

Force a player's view to the room they're in. They can't zoom out until you release it.

Players & voice

Mute: the room can't hear them. Deafen: they can't hear anyone. Direct: a private line with you from anywhere; while one is open only those players hear you.

    Move: WASD / arrows or click (on a phone: tap or use the joystick). H hides the controls. Click nearby doors and the fireplace to use them. Click a chair to sit or a bed to sleep. Connect your mic to talk: you're heard by people in the same room.
